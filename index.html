Work Process

As a self-taught developer, my journey began with a deep passion for learning and a drive to create. Over the course of a year, I honed my skills in web development by learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, PHP, and various other technologies. My work process reflects this growth and is centered around clear communication, a deep understanding of client needs, and a commitment to delivering high-quality, functional, and user-friendly websites.

1. Planning and Research

Once I’ve gathered all the necessary details, I begin planning. I research the latest trends in web design and development, ensuring that the website will be modern, responsive, and optimized for performance. I also consider the most appropriate technology stack based on the project requirements.

2. Testing and Debugging

Once the website is developed, I conduct rigorous testing. I test the site on different devices and browsers to ensure cross-browser compatibility. I also fix any bugs that may arise during testing and ensure the site is fully responsive.

3. Client Review and Feedback

OnI then present the site to the client, asking for feedback to ensure the site meets their expectations. I make necessary revisions and adjustments based on the client's input to deliver a website that aligns with their vision.

4. Launch and Support

After final approval, I launch the site and provide ongoing support to ensure smooth operation. I also offer training and documentation, empowering clients to manage their websites independently.
